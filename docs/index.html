What this page will and will not tell you
  • It will not print a price target. Gold cannot be forecast to a number, and a page that shows one is guessing with a straight face.
  • It shows distributions, not predictions. Given today's volatility and a century of precedent, here is the range gold has covered over 3, 6 and 12 months, with the odds attached.
  • Directional probabilities appear only if validated. Walk-forward tested against the base rate. If skill is indistinguishable from noise, the panel reads WITHHELD and shows the reasons.
  • Every figure names its source and date. Nothing is forward-filled or estimated. Missing data is shown as missing.
